# SOP-01: Incident triage

## 1. Acknowledge
Every critical alarm must be acknowledged in the NOC console within 15
minutes of raising. Acknowledgement assigns the incident to the on-shift
commander and starts the SLA clock.

## 2. Classify
Classify the incident by customer impact, not by alarm count: a single
critical alarm on a core element outranks fifty minor alarms on access
gear. Record the classification in the incident ticket before any
remediation begins.

## 3. Communicate
Post the first status update to the incident channel within 30 minutes.
Updates repeat every 60 minutes until the incident is resolved. Never
declare resolution from the NOC desk alone; confirm with the field tech
on site.
