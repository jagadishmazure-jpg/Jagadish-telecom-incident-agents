# SOP-04: SLA management

## 1. Know the targets
Restoration targets are 4 hours for critical, 8 for major, 24 for minor
and 72 for warning. The SLA clock starts at incident acknowledgement,
not at the first alarm.

## 2. Watch the clock
Any incident past 75% of its target is flagged at-risk and reviewed on
the next bridge. A breached SLA needs a written reason and a
corrective action before the ticket may close.

## 3. Credits need approval
SLA credits to customers are a finance decision, not a NOC decision.
Every credit request needs human confirmation with the breach evidence
attached; the agent may draft the request but never approves it.
