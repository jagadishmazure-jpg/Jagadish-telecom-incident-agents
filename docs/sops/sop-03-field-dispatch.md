# SOP-03: Field dispatch

## 1. Skill first
Dispatch only technicians who hold the required skill for the fault:
fiber-splicing for cuts, ip-routing for BGP, radio-rf for cell sites.
An unskilled dispatch is a wasted truck roll and is recorded as a miss.

## 2. Proximity and load
Among skilled techs, prefer the closest site, then the lowest current
load. Do not dispatch a tech already above 90% load unless no alternative
exists; record the override reason in the ticket.

## 3. Confirm arrival
The tech confirms arrival on site within the dispatch SLA. If no
confirmation arrives in 60 minutes, escalate to the regional dispatcher
and re-run the recommendation.
